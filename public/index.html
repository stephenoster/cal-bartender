Who's Collin?

Collin is an AI bartender with real opinions. He knows the classics cold, builds around what you actually have, and tells you straight what he'd make.

He's not a recipe database. Think of him as a craft bartender on call, for home bars that have outgrown the basics. He'll make you a great margarita when that's what you want, and he's even better at finding the drink you never knew you wanted.

Is Collin a real person?

No. Collin is an AI bartender. The opinions are his, the backstory is made up, and the recipes are the real thing.

What does it cost?

It's free. Your carrier's standard message and data rates still apply.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Collin is a regular phone number, so he works in the texting app you already use. If you'd rather type on a laptop, you can chat in your browser.

Why do you need my birthday?

Collin talks about alcohol, so he's only for people 21 and up. We check once when you sign up.

How often will he text me?

Mostly when you text him. He might follow up the same night to ask how a drink turned out, or check in now and then. No promos, no mass texts.

My bar isn't very stocked. Is this for me?

Yes. Tell him what you have and he'll work with it. Ask what to buy next and he'll tell you which bottle opens up the most drinks.

What happens to my number and email?

Your number is for texting with Collin, and we keep your conversation so he has context next time. Your email, if you give it, is only for news about Collin and seasonal recipes. Neither is ever sold or shared for marketing. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work outside the US?

Not yet. Texting works with US mobile numbers only. The browser chat works anywhere.

Who's behind this?

Ph Oster, a creative in Charlotte who loves tending bar at home. He built Collin for himself, to make better drinks and to learn more about AI along the way. If Collin makes your evening better, Ph would love to hear about it at hello at collinthomas dot bar.

How do I stop?

Reply STOP. You'll get one confirmation and nothing after that. Reply START if you want back in, or HELP if you need a hand. For email, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any message.
