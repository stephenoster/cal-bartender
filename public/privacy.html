Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Collin Thomas ("the Service") is operated as a personal project. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our cocktail recommendation service, by text message or in your browser.

What we collect

  • Your phone number
  • Your date of birth, to confirm you're 21 or older
  • Your email address, if you choose to give it
  • The content of messages you send to the Service
  • Conversation history, which may be stored to personalize future sessions and remember your preferences
  • A text list of the bottles and ingredients in bar photos you send, with a rough guess at how full each bottle is (not the photos themselves)

How we use it

Your phone number is used to text with you as Collin. Your date of birth is used only to confirm you're 21 or older. Your email address, if you give it, is used only to send occasional news about Collin and seasonal recipes. Email is separate from text messages, and you can unsubscribe from email at any time.

Your message content is used to generate personalized cocktail recommendations. Conversation history may be stored persistently to provide continuity across sessions, remember your preferences, and improve recommendations over time. We may also read conversations to fix problems and improve Collin.

Sub-processors

We use the following third-party services to operate:

  • Anthropic: AI processing of your messages and photos to generate recommendations
  • Telnyx: SMS delivery and phone number management
  • Railway: hosting and database storage for the Service
  • Email delivery provider: sending email updates to people who give us an email address

We do not sell, share, or distribute your information to any other third party. No mobile opt-in data, phone numbers, or email addresses will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Photos

If you send a photo of your bar or ingredients, Anthropic's API reads it so Collin can see what you're working with. We don't save the photo. We save a short text list of the bottles and ingredients it shows, with a rough guess at how full each bottle is, so Collin remembers your bar the next time you text. Send a new photo any time and the list updates. If a photo isn't of a bar, nothing from it is saved beyond a note in your conversation history that a photo was sent.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply START to resume. If you'd like your data deleted, email hello@collinthomas.bar and we'll take care of it. To stop email, use the unsubscribe link in any email or write to hello@collinthomas.bar.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email hello@collinthomas.bar or text the number directly.
